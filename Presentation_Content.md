# Presentation Content: SmartAbility 2026 Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Title
**Title:** CommuniAble AI
**Subtitle:** Empowering Workplace Communication for Persons with Disabilities
**Visual:** Clean, modern logo with diverse avatars interacting professionally.

## Slide 2: The Problem
**Header:** The Communication Gap in the Workplace
**Content:** 
- Many PwD face systemic barriers in professional communication scenarios (interviews, meetings, advocacy).
- Existing tools lack accessibility-first design and personalized feedback.
- Result: Underemployment and lack of career mobility.

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Header:** Introducing CommuniAble AI
**Content:** 
- An AI-powered training platform designed specifically for workplace scenarios.
- Features: Mock Interviews, Speech Assessment, and Workplace Roleplays.
- Built-in accessibility profiles to tailor the experience to neurodivergent, visually impaired, or hearing impaired users.

## Slide 4: Key Features (Learner)
**Header:** Tailored for the Individual
**Content:**
- **Real-time AI Feedback:** On tone, clarity, and professionalism.
- **Safe Environment:** Practice difficult conversations without judgment.
- **Adaptive Interfaces:** High contrast, screen-reader optimized, reduced motion.

## Slide 5: The Ecosystem
**Header:** Connecting Learners, Trainers, and Institutions
**Content:**
- **Trainers:** Monitor cohort progress, override AI feedback, build custom scenarios.
- **Institutions:** Track ROI, generate compliance reports, ensure inclusive practices.

## Slide 6: Technology & Accessibility
**Header:** Built on Inclusive Tech
**Content:**
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliant from day one.
- Advanced NLP models tuned to understand diverse speech patterns (including stutters/delays).

## Slide 7: Future Roadmap
**Header:** What's Next
**Content:**
- Integration with major HR platforms.
- Expansion of scenario library to specialized industries.
- VR workplace simulations.
