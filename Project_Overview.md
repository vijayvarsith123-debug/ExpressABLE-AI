# Project Overview: CommuniAble AI

## Executive Summary
CommuniAble AI is an "AI-powered Workplace Communication Training Platform for Persons with Disabilities." Traditional e-learning platforms fail to accommodate the nuanced, dynamic communication needs of individuals with disabilities in professional settings. CommuniAble AI fills this gap by leveraging local and cloud-based AI to provide realistic workplace simulations, speech and fluency analysis, and accessible contextual learning, equipping users with the confidence and skills needed for workplace success.

## Problem Statement
Persons with Disabilities (PwD) face significant barriers in workplace communication, leading to underemployment and lack of career progression. Generic communication tools and standard e-learning platforms are not tailored to address specific disabilities (e.g., speech impediments, cognitive processing delays, visual impairments). There is a critical need for an adaptive platform that assesses current capabilities and provides personalized, real-time feedback in simulated professional scenarios.

## Business Case
By addressing the communication training needs of PwD, CommuniAble AI taps into a significantly underserved market. The platform offers immense value to:
- **Individuals**: Enhancing employability and workplace confidence.
- **Institutions & NGOs**: Providing scalable, measurable training tools for their beneficiaries.
- **Corporations**: Supporting inclusive hiring initiatives and employee resource groups.

## Target Users & Accessibility Focus
The platform is designed with specific adaptations for the following disabilities:
- **Hearing Disabilities**: Visual feedback, transcripts, clear visual cues.
- **Speech Disabilities**: Nuanced speech analysis, patience in processing, text-to-speech alternatives.
- **Cognitive & Learning Disabilities**: Simple language mode, social stories, adaptive pacing, contextual cues.
- **Visual Disabilities**: Screen reader optimization (WCAG 2.2 AAA), voice navigation, high-contrast modes.

## Platform-Level Success Metrics
- **User Engagement**: Frequency of daily and weekly goal completions.
- **Skill Improvement**: Measurable improvement in AI-assessed communication scores over time.
- **Accessibility Adoption**: Percentage of users utilizing custom accessibility profiles.
- **Institutional Adoption**: Number of trainers and institutions actively monitoring user progress.
- **System Performance**: Latency of AI feedback (targeting < 2 seconds for local inference).
