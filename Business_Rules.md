# Business Rules

## 1. Accessibility Enforcement
- **BR1.1**: Accessibility preferences chosen during onboarding must be automatically applied across all sessions and devices upon login.
- **BR1.2**: A user can change their accessibility profile at any time from a persistent, highly visible header button.

## 2. Constructive Evaluation
- **BR2.1**: Speech and written assessments must NEVER display the word "Fail" or use red as a primary indicator of error (to accommodate cognitive sensitivities and color blindness).
- **BR2.2**: AI feedback MUST always begin with positive reinforcement before offering constructive suggestions.

## 3. Assessment Retries
- **BR3.1**: Users may retry any simulation, mock interview, or speech assessment an unlimited number of times.
- **BR3.2**: Only the highest score or most improved session counts toward the weekly progress goals.

## 4. Trainer Authority
- **BR4.1**: Trainer feedback and manual overrides explicitly supersede AI-generated recommendations and scores.
- **BR4.2**: If a Trainer overrides an AI score, the system flags the interaction for internal review to improve future AI accuracy.

## 5. Data Privacy and Institutional Access
- **BR5.1**: Institutions (Administrators) can only view aggregated, anonymized reports. They cannot view individual user transcripts or voice recordings.
- **BR5.2**: Trainers can only view data for Learners explicitly assigned to them.
