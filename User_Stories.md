# User Stories

## Learner Stories

**US-L01: Accessibility Onboarding**
- **Given** I am a new Learner with a visual impairment
- **When** I log into the platform for the first time
- **Then** I am prompted to set my Accessibility Profile (e.g., High Contrast, Screen Reader optimization) so the UI adapts to my needs immediately.

**US-L02: Mock Interview Practice**
- **Given** I am a Learner preparing for a job
- **When** I start a Mock Interview simulation
- **Then** the AI acts as the interviewer and provides a realistic, constructive conversation without pass/fail grading.

**US-L03: Speech Analysis**
- **Given** I am a Learner with a speech impediment
- **When** I use the Speech Analysis tool
- **Then** the system provides patient, non-judgmental feedback on my pronunciation and pacing, allowing unlimited retries.

**US-L04: Simple Language Mode**
- **Given** I am a Learner with a cognitive disability
- **When** I toggle 'Simple Language Mode'
- **Then** all complex instructions and AI feedback are simplified to a basic reading level.

**US-L05: Progress Tracking**
- **Given** I am a Learner
- **When** I view my Progress Dashboard
- **Then** I see my Communication Score trends and completed daily goals represented visually.

## Trainer Stories

**US-T01: Monitor Learner Progress**
- **Given** I am a Trainer
- **When** I access the Trainer Dashboard
- **Then** I can view the recent simulation results and feedback scores of assigned Learners.

**US-T02: Override AI Feedback**
- **Given** I am a Trainer reviewing a Learner's simulation
- **When** I disagree with the AI's assessment
- **Then** I can manually override the feedback and provide my own constructive notes.

**US-T03: Assign Specific Scenarios**
- **Given** I am a Trainer
- **When** I know a Learner is struggling with asking for help
- **Then** I can assign a specific 'Workplace Simulation' focused on that topic to their dashboard.

## Institution Administrator Stories

**US-I01: Aggregated Reporting**
- **Given** I am an Institution Administrator
- **When** I view the Institution Dashboard
- **Then** I see aggregated metrics (usage, average improvement) for all users under my institution to evaluate program success.

**US-I02: User Management**
- **Given** I am an Institution Administrator
- **When** I access the user management panel
- **Then** I can invite Trainers and allocate Learner licenses.

*(Note: Document contains 10 representative user stories from the 30+ backlog for brevity in MVP planning)*
